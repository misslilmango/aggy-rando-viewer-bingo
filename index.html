How to Play & Board Options

  • Please do not use your card as an excuse to backseat or spam in chat to influence gameplay.
  • Randomizer Modes: These pools contain items tailored specifically to tracking LilAggy's weekly randomizer gameplay.
  • Elden Ring Bingo Mode: This pool is universal and built to work for ANY streamer matchup, including Bingo Brawlers events. Generic text indicators like "Gets first square" apply to the streamer you are currently rooting for.
  • Meta Bingo Mode: Tracks board coordinates rather than in-game events. When a tile is claimed during the stream, find and mark that coordinate on your board.
  • Boards Reset at 6AM EST/EDT Daily: If you need a new card before the reset, append a number to your username (e.g., username1) and regenerate the board.
